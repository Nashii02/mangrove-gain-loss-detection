Quick Help & Guidance
What does this system do?

This tool monitors coastal mangrove forests along Barangay Dulao, Aringay, La Union from 2019 to 2024 using satellite photography. It helps the Aringay MENRO track forest recovery and prioritize field inspections.

Understanding map categories:
● Stable Mangrove
Present in both years.
▲ Mapped Gain
Newly grown trees.
▼ Mapped Loss
Disappeared/removed.
◻ Unchanged
Non-mangrove land.
Common Questions:
• What should MENRO do if "Mapped Loss" is detected?
Deploy barangay field officers to the flagged coordinates to inspect whether loss was caused by storm waves, erosion, cutting, or tidal shifts.
• How big is one hectare?
1 hectare equals 10,000 square meters, or approximately 1.4 standard football fields.
• How do I download an official report?
Visit the Reports tab to generate and download a formal municipal PDF report ready for signature.
